Three moves. Under a second.

Try each one below. The 3D scene reacts to what you do.

01Phone · Controller

Select, then pinch and hold

Pick an asset from the dock on your phone. Bring thumb and index finger together and hold for 0.35 seconds. The hand skeleton turns the asset's colour and a haptic buzz confirms it's charged.

Hold the button the way you'd hold the pinch. Let go early and it resets.

Waiting for a pinch.

02Release · Flick

Flick toward the screen

Release the pinch with a quick forward or upward flick. A velocity guard ignores slow drifts, so nothing launches by accident. When a flick passes, the throw reaches the stage over WebSockets in under 20 ms.

velocity guard

Send a motion through the guard.

03Desktop · Display Stage

Materialize, then pull it back

A 3D shockwave rolls across the desktop stage as the asset arrives. To take it back, reach toward the screen, pinch, and pull back. The asset returns to your phone.
